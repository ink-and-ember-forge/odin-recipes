Lasagna

Description

Prepare this easy homemade lasagna ahead of time and save int he freezer, uncooked, for when you need it during a busy week. Just bake for an extra 45 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 750g lean mince
  • 90g pack prosciutto
  • 800g passata
  • 200ml hot beef stock
  • nutmeg
  • 300g fresh lasagna sheets
  • white sauce
  • 125g ball mozzarella

Steps

  1. To make the meat sauce, heat 2tbsp olive oil in a frying pan and cook 750g lean beef mince in two batches for about 10 minutes until browned all over.
  2. Finely chop 4 clises of prsociutto from a 90g pack, then stir through the meat mixture.
  3. Pour over 800g passata and 200ml hot beef stock. Add a little grated nutmeg, then season.
  4. Bring up to the boil, then simmer for 30 mins until the sauce looks rich.
  5. Heat the oven to 180C/160C fan and lightly oil an overproof dish.
  6. Spoon one third of the meat sauce into the dish, then cover with some fresh lasagna sheets from a 300g pacl. Drizzle over roughly 130g ready-made white sauce.
  7. Repeat until you have three layers of pasta. Cover with the remaining 390g white sauce, making sure you can't see any pasta poking through.
  8. Scatter 125g torn mozzarella over the top.
  9. Arrange the rest of the prosciutto on top. Bake for 45 mins until the top is bubbling and lightly browned.
